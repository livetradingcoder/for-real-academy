Refund Policy

Last updated: October 2026

14-day refund

If you've completed less than 30% of a course, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase. Email hello@forrealtech.dev with the email used at checkout. No reason needed.

Instalment plans

If you refund within 14 days, any instalments paid are returned and future instalments are cancelled. After 14 days you can cancel at any time to stop future instalments; payments already made are not refunded.

Timing

Approved refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

Consumer rights

Nothing here limits statutory rights you may have where you live.
