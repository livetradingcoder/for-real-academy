About

A software studio that teaches

For Real Academy is the training arm of For Real Tech LLC, a Wyoming software studio that builds web apps and integrations for clients. The courses teach what we use day to day: modern web basics and practical, careful use of AI tools.

What you get: recorded lessons, written notes, exercises and a project in each course. What you don't get: promises of a job or income. We say what a course covers, and we'll refund you if it isn't right (see the refund policy).

FAQ

Questions

Do I need experience?

No. Beginner courses assume none. The AI web apps course expects you to be comfortable with the basics from the first course or equivalent.

How do instalments work?

You pay the first instalment at checkout and the next two on the same day of the following months. Access starts after the first payment.

What is your refund policy?

Full refund within 14 days of purchase if you've completed less than 30% of the course. See the refund policy.

Will this get me a job or make me money?

We can't promise that. The courses teach skills; outcomes depend on your effort and circumstances.

Who runs this?

For Real Academy is operated by For Real Tech LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company. Contact: hello@forrealtech.dev.

Do you teach trading or financial strategies?

No. These courses cover web development and AI tools only.

Questions before you enroll?

Email hello@forrealtech.dev. We reply within two business days.
