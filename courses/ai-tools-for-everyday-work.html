AI Tools for Everyday Work

Use AI assistants well: write better prompts, check the output, and build repeatable workflows for writing, research, spreadsheets and email.

LevelBeginner
Length6 modules · about 6 hours
FormatSelf-paced, recorded

What you'll learn

  • Write clear prompts and iterate on results
  • Verify AI output and spot mistakes
  • Automate routine writing and research tasks
  • Handle privacy and data safely
  • Build a personal prompt library

Curriculum

  1. How AI assistants work and where they fail
  2. Prompting: context, examples, constraints, iteration
  3. Writing and editing with AI without losing your voice
  4. Research and summarising: checking sources and facts
  5. Spreadsheets, email and repeatable workflows
  6. Privacy, safety and responsible use

Who it's for

Professionals, freelancers and students who want to use AI assistants productively and carefully.

What you'll need

An account with any AI assistant (free tiers work). No technical background needed.

What you'll finish with

A personal prompt library and three workflows you use in your own work.
