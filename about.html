About

A software studio that teaches

For Real Academy is the training arm of For Real Tech LLC, a Wyoming software studio that builds web apps and integrations for clients. The courses teach what we use day to day: modern web basics and careful, practical use of AI tools.

What we teach

Web development and AI tools, and nothing else. We don't teach trading, investing or financial strategies.

How we approach it

  • Build real things. Each course ends with something you made and shipped.
  • Honest about outcomes. We teach skills. We don't promise jobs, income or results, and we say what each course does and doesn't cover.
  • Low risk. Pay once or in three monthly payments, with a 14-day refund window.
